Art Asset Credits
=================

The environment/background art under assets/img/ comes from Kenney
(https://kenney.nl), released under the Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
public-domain dedication. No attribution is legally required, but credit
is given gladly.

Packs used (curated subsets):
  - Platformer Art Deluxe   -> ground/ grass + dirt tiles
  - Background Elements      -> sky/ clouds, hills, mountains
  - Foliage Pack             -> foliage/ trees, bushes, grass tufts, flowers
  - Pattern Pack             -> pattern/ wallpaper motifs (recoloured in-engine)
  - Platformer Characters    -> char/ human NPC sprites (see below)

License: CC0 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Human NPCs use Kenney "Platformer Characters" poses, some shirt-recoloured
in-engine (a one-time hue swap that preserves the original shading):
  - Dog Catcher  = Soldier (idle/walk), shirt recoloured BLUE (uniform) + a
                   hand-drawn net — deliberately unlike anyone in the family
  - Campers      = Adventurer / Player (idle/cheer)
  - Lily         = Female, shirt recoloured ORANGE
  - Leia         = Female, shirt recoloured GOLD
  - Mom          = Female, shirt recoloured PURPLE  (homecoming screen)
  - Dad          = Adventurer, shirt recoloured ORANGE (homecoming screen)
Name tags keep Lily/Leia distinct.  The same sprites appear in-game and on the
homecoming screen so the cast looks consistent everywhere.

Ollie himself and all the animals/creatures (lion, hawk, bird, house cat,
Roomba, the angel, the magic carpet, the bone) remain original, hand-coded
procedural artwork drawn at runtime — the heart of the game stays the
family's own.
